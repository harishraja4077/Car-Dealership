Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need for financing?

You'll need a valid government ID, proof of income (recent pay stubs or bank statements), proof of residence, and your social security number. Our finance team will guide you through the process.

Can I trade in my current vehicle?

Absolutely! We offer competitive trade-in valuations. Bring your vehicle for a quick 15-minute appraisal, and we'll apply its value directly toward your new purchase.

Do you offer extended warranties?

Yes, we offer flexible extended warranty plans covering up to 100,000 miles. Choose from bumper-to-bumper or powertrain coverage depending on your needs.

How long does a test drive take?

We recommend allowing 30-45 minutes for a test drive. You'll get a full route experience covering highways, city streets, and even a parking area to test maneuverability.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

We offer a 7-day/500-mile money-back guarantee on all certified pre-owned vehicles, giving you complete peace of mind with every purchase.
